Our Story

Heritage, woven forward

Sri Govardhan Silks has been a trusted name in silk sarees at Kattigenahalli, Yelahanka, Bengaluru. Rooted in the weaving traditions of South India, every piece we offer carries the spirit of artisanal mastery — from selecting the finest mulberry silk to the final fold. We believe that a saree is not merely fabric; it is heritage, emotion, and art — woven together for generations to come.
