THE ARTISANAL JOURNEY

From Thread to Masterpiece

The sacred 5-stage transformation of raw South Indian mulberry silk into an enduring family heirloom.

01 PHASE I

Mulberry Silk

Hand-selected high-twist mulberry silk yarns chosen for natural tensile strength, unbroken filament length, and pure luster.

02 PHASE II

Natural Dyeing

Vibrant botanical and mineral pigments drawn slowly into individual yarn baths by hand, ensuring depth that defies fading.

03 PHASE III

Pit Loom Weaving

Interlacing warp and weft on traditional wooden pit looms with precision foot-pedal rhythm, taking up to 20 days per saree.

04 PHASE IV

Pure Zari Inlay

Intricate gold and silver metallic ribbons worked meticulously into borders and pallus using traditional jacquard punch cards.

05 PHASE V

Artisanal Finish

Thread-by-thread inspection, hand-steam pressing, and museum-grade folding ready for your most sacred occasions.

OUR HERITAGE

Heritage, Woven Forward

Rooted in the timeless weaving heritage of South India, Sri Govardhan Silks is a celebrated silk saree manufacturer and boutique located at Kattigenahalli, Yelahanka, Bengaluru.

We believe that a pure silk saree is never just fabric—it is an heirloom of devotion, a sanctuary of handloom culture, and an enduring symbol of grace passed from mother to daughter. Every saree in our showroom is directly sourced from master weavers, preserving authentic craftsmanship without compromise.
