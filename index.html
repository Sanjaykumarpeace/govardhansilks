THE KORVAI TECHNIQUE
HERITAGE DEFINED • THE ALCHEMY OF SILK

Two Hearts, One Rhythm

The Korvai technique is the pinnacle of Kanchipuram weaving, joining a solid color border with a contrasting body. It requires two master weavers operating in perfect synchrony—a delicate dance of passing shuttles resulting in a seamless, indestructible bond.

MATERIAL INTEGRITY • PURE ZARI

Pure Gold Zari

True luxury is uncompromising. Our Zari is crafted using the time-honored method: a core of fine red silk thread, wrapped in silver wire, and finally electroplated in pure 24-karat gold for an heirloom luster that matures beautifully over generations.

OUR HERITAGE

Heritage, Woven Forward

Rooted in the timeless weaving heritage of South India, Sri Govardhan Silks is a celebrated silk saree manufacturer and boutique located at Kattigenahalli, Yelahanka, Bengaluru.

We believe that a pure silk saree is never just fabric—it is an heirloom of devotion, a sanctuary of handloom culture, and an enduring symbol of grace passed from mother to daughter. Every saree in our showroom is directly sourced from master weavers, preserving authentic craftsmanship without compromise.
